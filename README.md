# 🚀 Strategic & Tactical Initiatives Hub

## 📌 Executive Summary
The primary aim of this strategic and tactical initiative hub is to follow on the implementation of various initiatives geard towards creating a digitally driven and seamless banking service provision by the end of 2028.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Pillars (2026)
*High-level focus areas that drive our long-term vision.*

1. **Pillar 1: Digitalization** – increase digital transaction rate to 65% from its base.
2. **Pillar 2: Business Growth** - resource generation of both deposit and FCY.
3. **Pillar 3: People & Culture** - enhance the engagement and productivity of employees.
4. **Pillar 4: Customer-Centricity** – Expanding market share through targeted feature delivery and engagement.
5. **Pillar 5: Cost Optimization** – implement cost saving initiatives and create organization wide awareness.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Success Metrics
We measure the impact of our tactical execution against these core organizational metrics:

*   **Process Efficiency:** Reduce time-to-completion for core administrative workflows by **25%**.
*   **System Reliability:** Maintain zero data loss and target **99.9%** availability during peak processing cycles.
*   **User Adoption:** Achieve a **90%** active engagement rate across target segments within 60 days of deployment.

### 🏷️ Issue Labeling Conventions
When creating or filtering issues, use the following standardized labels to maintain alignment:
*   `pillar: operational-excellence` — Tasks tied to workflow efficiency.
*   `pillar: customer-growth` — Tasks tied to user-facing enhancements.
*   `type: tactical-initiative` — High-level epics tracking a major strategic block.
*   `priority: high` / `priority: medium` — Immediate vs. secondary execution focus.
